01 — Brand Story

More than ice cream.

We believe in the freedom to be weird, to be bold, to choose what makes you happy. CHOFTI is the brand that doesn't take itself too seriously — because ice cream shouldn't either.

We're playful but never careless. Clever but never cold. And we always, always Choose our CHOFTI.

05 — Blob System

Shape with a soul.

Our blobs aren't decorations — they're a core part of the visual language. Organic, fluid, and full of personality.

Style 01

Expressive Blobs

High contrast. Used for highlights, hero sections, and emotional peaks. Bold, attention-grabbing. Max 20% of any layout.

Style 02

Subtle Blobs

Tonal. Same-family colors — slightly lighter or darker. Used as background depth, framing, and texture. At least 80% of blob usage.

↗

Flow in one directionBlobs should appear to move consistently — top-right, bottom-left, etc. Never scatter randomly.

◱

Always partially croppedBlobs should bleed off the edges of their container. Never fully contained inside a frame.

80

80/20 rule80% subtle blobs for depth. 20% expressive blobs for impact. This keeps layouts from feeling chaotic.
